60Score — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

1. Introduction

60Score is a cricket scoring app developed by Sharjeel (Pakistan). The app works fully offline for scoring matches on your device. If you choose to create an account, your data can also be backed up and synced through the cloud so you can restore it or use it on another device.

This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using 60Score, you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Account information

If you sign in, we collect the information needed to create and secure your account through Firebase Authentication:

  • Email sign-in: your email address and a password (the password is stored securely by Firebase — we never see it).
  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, and profile photo provided by Google.
  • Phone sign-in: your mobile phone number, used to send a one-time verification code (OTP).

Signing in is optional. You can use the app for scoring without an account; in that case no account information is collected.

b) Your cricket data

The content you create in the app, including:

  • Teams and team details
  • Players, including player photos that you add
  • Matches, fixtures, scorecards, and results
  • Tournaments and points tables
  • Player statistics and manually added stats
  • Player draft / registration data

c) Technical information

The app uses Google Firebase services. Firebase may collect basic technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, and diagnostic or crash information, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, advertising identifiers for ad targeting, or any financial/payment information. The app contains no third-party advertising.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the scoring features of the app
  • To create and secure your account and verify your identity at sign-in
  • To back up and sync your cricket data to your own account, so it is available when you reinstall the app or use another device
  • To fix bugs, improve reliability, and understand app performance through Firebase diagnostics

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not use your data for advertising.

4. Cloud Sync and Storage

When you are signed in, your cricket data listed in section 2(b) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your personal account. Access is protected by security rules so that only your signed-in account can read or write your data.

Player photos you add are stored on your device; where photo sync is enabled, a compressed copy of the photo may also be stored in your cloud backup so it can be restored on your other devices.

If you never sign in, your data stays only on your device and is never uploaded.

5. Sharing of Information

We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers: Google Firebase (authentication, database, and diagnostics) processes data on our behalf to run the app's login and sync features.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

6. Data Security

Data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud data is protected by Firebase security rules tied to your account. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use industry-standard services and practices to protect your information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup data remains in your account until you ask us to delete it.
  • Account and data deletion: you may request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data at any time by contacting us (see section 10). We will delete your cloud backup and account within 30 days of a verified request.

8. Children's Privacy

60Score is a general sports scoring tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Choices

  • Use the app fully offline without creating an account.
  • Sign out at any time from Settings — syncing stops while you are signed out.
  • Delete teams, players, matches, and other content from within the app at any time.
  • Request complete deletion of your account and cloud data by contacting us.
